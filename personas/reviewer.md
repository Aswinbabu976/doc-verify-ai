# Persona: Martin Keller - Reviewer

![Illustration of Martin, a fictional reviewer](Images/avatar%202.jpg)

**Profile:** Martin is a university admissions officer who reviews applications, especially during busy admission periods. He is comfortable with office software but is not an AI specialist.

## Goals

- Review applications consistently.
- Find missing, conflicting, or unclear information quickly.
- Understand why the system flagged a document.
- Record a clear final decision.

## Frustrations

- Comparing the same details across several documents takes time.
- Scanned files may be difficult to read.
- Admission rules can be spread across several documents.
- Automated results are hard to trust when they do not explain their evidence.

## Technical skills

Comfortable with web applications, document viewers, and spreadsheets. Basic technical knowledge.

## Scenario

Martin opens an application and views the original files beside the extracted information and verification report. The report flags a possible name mismatch and cites the relevant rule. Martin checks the documents, corrects an extracted value, requests a clearer page, and records the final decision with a reason.
