# Persona: Lina Hassan - Applicant

**Profile:** Lina is an international student applying to a master's program. She uses a phone and laptop comfortably but is new to the university's application system.

## Goals

- Submit all required documents correctly.
- Understand which documents are missing or unclear.
- Check her application status without repeatedly contacting admissions.

## Frustrations

- Upload instructions are unclear.
- She is unsure which file formats are accepted.
- She does not know why a document must be submitted again.
- Status updates are slow or difficult to find.

## Technical skills

Comfortable with email, web forms, PDF files, and uploading documents. Not familiar with internal admissions systems.

## Scenario

Lina creates an application and uploads a transcript and an English language certificate. She adds a note explaining a spelling difference in her name. DocVerify AI confirms which files were received and shows the application status. If a page is unreadable  it asks her to upload a clearer copy.
