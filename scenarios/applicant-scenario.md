# Scenario: Applicant submits documents

Lina is applying to a master's program. She signs in, creates an application, and selects the required document types. She uploads her transcript and English-language certificate, then adds a note explaining that her name is spelled differently on one document.

DocVerify AI confirms that the files were received and checks whether they can be read. One page is unclear, so the system marks it for attention and asks Lina to upload a clearer copy. Lina can see the application status and the documents still needed.
